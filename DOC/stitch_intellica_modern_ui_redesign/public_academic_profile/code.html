Biography

Dr. Eleanor Vance is a leading researcher in the intersection of artificial intelligence and cognitive neuroscience. With over 15 years of experience in academia, her work focuses on developing computational models to understand synaptic plasticity and learning mechanisms in the human brain. She has previously held positions at the National Institute of Cognitive Health and has been a visiting scholar at multiple international institutions.
